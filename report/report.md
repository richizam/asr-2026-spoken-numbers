## 1. Problem statement

Russian spoken-numbers ASR. Given an audio clip of someone reading an integer
in the range [1 000, 999 999], predict the integer. Primary metric is the
harmonic mean of CER across in-domain (inD) and out-of-domain (ooD) speakers;
ooD CER is the tie-breaker.

Hard constraints: train from scratch, ≤ 5 M parameters, input 16 kHz,
external data allowed only for noise augmentation.

## 2. Data

| Split | #utts | #speakers | samplerate | format |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| train | 12 553 | 6 | 22.05 / 24 kHz | wav |
| dev   |  2 265 | 10 (4 OOD) | 16 kHz | ~70% wav / ~30% mp3 |
| test  |  2 582 | 14 | 16 kHz | ~70% wav / ~30% mp3 |

Train is heavily skewed — `spk_E` alone accounts for ~45 % of the training
set. Dev contains 4 speakers unseen in train; test contains ~8 fully unseen
voices. Roughly 90 % of all transcriptions are 6-digit numbers.

## 3. Approach

### 3.1 Label representation

We predict **Russian orthographic text** (characters + space), not digits.
Targets are generated by `num2words(n, lang='ru')`, e.g.

> `139 473 → "сто тридцать девять тысяч четыреста семьдесят три"`

Vocabulary: 33 Cyrillic letters + space + CTC blank = 35 tokens.

At inference, we parse the decoded string back to an integer with a
deterministic Russian-numeral parser. If the string contains unknown tokens
(typos), each token is snapped to the closest legal number-word by
Levenshtein distance, then re-parsed. A final range clamp to
[1 000, 999 999] guarantees every output is a legal submission value.

### 3.2 Architecture

Conformer-CTC small:

| Block | Spec |
|---|---|
| Front-end | 80-dim log-mel, 25 ms win / 10 ms hop |
| Subsampling | 2× Conv2d stride-2 → 4× time reduction |
| Encoder | 12 × Conformer (d_model=128, FFN=256, 4 heads, conv_k=15) |
| Head | Linear → 35 classes (CTC) |

Positional encoding is standard sinusoidal (absolute) — saves ~0.5 M params
vs. relative-positional attention. Total parameter count: **3 108 227**
(under the 5 M cap with comfortable headroom).

### 3.3 Augmentations

- Speed perturbation 0.9 / 1.0 / 1.1
- Pitch shift ±2 semitones + VTLP α ∈ [0.9, 1.1]
- MP3 re-encoding @ 32–128 kbps (closes train↔dev/test codec gap)
- MUSAN additive noise @ SNR 5–20 dB
- Small-RIR convolutional reverb
- Gain ±10 dB
- SpecAugment on log-mel (2×F=15, 2×T=35)
- **Speaker-balanced WeightedRandomSampler** to counter `spk_E` dominance

### 3.4 Training recipe

- AdamW, peak lr 1e-3, 2 000-step linear warm-up, cosine decay
- fp16 autocast, batch 16, grad-accum 2 → effective 32
- Grad clip 1.0, weight decay 1e-4, dropout 0.1
- ~120 epochs, early stop on dev harmonic-mean CER
- Last-5 checkpoint averaging
- Per-`spk_id` dev CER logged every epoch

### 3.5 Decoding

Three-stage decoder:
1. CTC prefix beam search (width 16) on log-softmax.
2. Shallow fusion with a 3-gram KenLM (char-level or word-level) built over
   `num2words(n, 'ru')` for n ∈ [1 000, 999 999]. Fusion weight α ≈ 0.4,
   word-insertion β ≈ 1.0.
3. Trie snap / word-level snap → integer in [1 000, 999 999].
